## TL;DR

Atlassian – large product company behind Jira, Confluence, Trello, Loom and other teamwork tools; the open role is in Audit Platform and Guard, the security and data-governance layer, bringing security and audit experiences to native iOS. Technical match is high and the reliability/security focus fits the profile. The one hard unknown: Team Anywhere requires a country where Atlassian has a legal entity – Belarus availability is unconfirmed and must be answered before investing interview time.

## Product and market

- Products named on the careers site: Rovo, Jira, Jira Align, Jira Service Management, Confluence, Loom (job page footer, 2026-10-20).
- The Guard/Audit Platform team: "the security and data-governance layer for Atlassian" – centralized controls and visibility for admins, protecting sensitive data, governance and risk management at scale; the mobile mission is to let customers "monitor activity, investigate issues, and stay in control wherever they are" (job posting).
- Market position, revenue, competitors: no data verified in this research (public company facts not checked this session).

## Technology and stack

- From the JD: Swift, native iOS, test-driven development (XCTest, XCUITest), REST/GraphQL APIs, APNs, CI/CD with Fastlane and Bitrise, performance work (launch time, memory, network), crash monitoring, some frontend web stack work.
- Android experience is "a plus but not required" – cross-platform awareness valued.
- Engineering culture: the Atlassian Engineering handbook describes autonomous teams built on "ownership, trust, and a common language" ([atlassian.com/blog/how-we-build/handbook](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/how-we-build/handbook)).
- Specific iOS architecture, library choices, blog posts by the mobile team: no data found.

## Team and culture

- Team Anywhere (since 2020): employees choose office, home, or a mix; work is possible from any country where Atlassian has a legal entity, with sufficient timezone overlap with the team, team lead approval and legal right to work in the region ([globalcareers-atlassian.icims.com quote](https://globalcareers-atlassian.icims.com/company/careers/teamanywhere), [kadence.co, 03.04.2025](https://kadence.co/news/why-atlassian-is-sticking-with-distributed-work/)).
- Reported scale: ~12,000 employees (kadence.co, 03.04.2025).
- Benefits reported by aggregators: 26 weeks paid leave for birthing parents, 20 weeks for non-birthing parents (4dayweek.io – aggregator data, treat as secondary).
- Team '27 conference announced in San Francisco (events.atlassian.com/team).
- Glassdoor/DOU reviews, mobile team size: no data collected.

## Flags

🟢 High technical match: Swift/iOS core requirements map almost 1:1 to the profile (7+ years, architecture patterns, REST, APNs, CI/CD incl. Bitrise, XCTest).
🟢 Security, reliability and sensitive-data focus matches the reliability track record (99.8% crash-free on 160K+ DAU; access-management domain).
🟢 Team Anywhere and distributed, cross-functional collaboration fit the target format (remote work from Belarus).
🔴 Team Anywhere country rule: needs an Atlassian legal entity in the employee's country. Belarus is unconfirmed and likely unsupported – this is a stop-or-go question before spending interview time. Contract/EOR alternatives: unknown.
🔴 Compensation not published in the JD; the profile floor is 4500 USD gross/month – likely fine for Atlassian, but the actual range for this location must be confirmed.
🟡 GraphQL and XCUITest appear in requirements but are not in the CV (partial gaps).

## Questions to ask them
