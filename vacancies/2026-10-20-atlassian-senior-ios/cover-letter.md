Guard is the part of Atlassian I find most concrete: the security and data-governance layer where admins get centralized controls and visibility, and where leaders can safely push AI adoption. The mobile side of that mission – letting customers monitor activity, investigate issues and stay in control from anywhere – is exactly the product surface I want to work on: security functionality that has to feel instant and trustworthy on a phone.

My background is 7+ years of native iOS with Swift: architecture patterns, REST APIs, APNs, CI/CD and XCTest. In my current project I own business-critical flows for a platform serving 160K+ DAU and contribute to a 99.8% crash-free rate; earlier I shipped permission and guest-access flows for an application serving 500K+ DAU. I also raised unit test coverage from 13% to 75% to keep regressions out of releases. The transferable skill for Guard is handling sensitive data in access-management products: permissions, restrictions and error states that users must trust.

The challenge I see: audit and security UX is dense, and on mobile it must stay fast while feature parity with web is kept across teams. One idea is to make reliability part of the security experience itself: a performance budget and crash-free goals for the mobile audit flows, backed by validated event tracking so investigations run on trustworthy data. I would be glad to discuss how I could help with that.
